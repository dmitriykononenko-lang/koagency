Getting started

  1. Install the widget and open its settings.
  2. On the Settings tab, set the client's business hours and days.
  3. To filter by country, create contact fields and select them on the Fields tab.
  4. On the Database tab, click Check database, then Fill contacts. Keep the tab open until it finishes.
