1. Summary

The widget runs in the user's browser inside the amoCRM account. Contact data is not sent to the operator's servers and is not stored by the operator. The only thing sent to the operator is the contact phone the administrator enters in the widget settings.

2. Data the widget uses

  • Contact phone numbers: to determine the country, time zone, region, city and carrier.
  • Values of selected contact fields: so that fields already filled in are not overwritten.
  • The account's list of contact fields: to show them in the settings.
  • Widget settings: business hours and selected fields. They are stored in the amoCRM account.

2.1. Contact phone

On installation the administrator enters a contact phone: the widget turns on only after that. By entering the phone, the administrator agrees to its processing for the purposes below. When the settings are saved, the phone together with the amoCRM account ID and address is sent to the operator's server (Selectel, Moscow) and stored in the operator's CRM. The operator uses it to contact you about the widget, tell you about updates and help with setup. The phone is not shared with third parties. The operator's server also technically processes the request IP address. 30 days after the widget is disabled, the installation record is deleted from the operator's server. The phone is kept in the operator's CRM until you withdraw your consent or ask for the number to be deleted. On such a request to hello@koagency.me, the operator deletes the phone within 10 business days.

3. How it works

The number is matched against tables included in the widget archive. Fields are written through the amoCRM API on behalf of the current user, and only the fields an administrator selected in the settings. No data is passed to third-party services.

4. Account access

On installation amoCRM sends a one-time code to the integration address. The operator does not exchange it for tokens and gains no account access through it. The phone is sent with a one-time amoCRM signature that the operator's server uses to verify the account; the signature gives no access to account data. The widget's pages do not use cookies.

Page hosting

The privacy, support and connection pages are hosted by Vercel Inc. (USA). When they are opened, the provider technically processes the IP address and the request address, which carries the one-time code during installation. Contact data is not sent to these pages.

5. Support requests

If you contact support, the operator receives what you include in your message and uses it only to reply. To have your correspondence deleted, write to the address above.

6. Removal

Disable the widget in the amoCRM account and it stops working. Values it wrote to contact fields remain in your account and can be removed with amoCRM tools. To have your contact phone deleted by the operator or to withdraw your consent, write to hello@koagency.me. We delete it within 10 business days, as described in section 2.1.

7. Accuracy

Country and region come from the numbering plan and show where the number was issued, not where the person is now.

8. Changes

New versions are published on this page. If the widget ever starts sending other data to the operator, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
